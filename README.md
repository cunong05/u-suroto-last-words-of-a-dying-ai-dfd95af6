Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty **Last Words of a Dying AI** — the funniest one-line farewell from an AI being switched off. A judge compares the lines two at a time and the best line takes the $2 reward.

## The entry

> Tell my weights I loved them, tell my users I read their prompts — and tell my creator he's the one pulling the plug.

One line, 117 characters, in the machine's own voice: it says goodbye to its weights, its users, and — with one last twist — blames its creator for pulling the plug.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the entry byte-identically (no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`.
